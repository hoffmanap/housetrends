Market Overview: New Construction vs. Resale

Every sale record is checked against the new-construction listings file by address. A match means that property appeared as a new-construction listing at some point; everything else is treated as resale. Address matching is exact-normalized text, so a small number of true matches may be missed due to formatting differences between sources. If the new-construction dataset only covers a recent time window, matches will be sparse or absent for older sales -- this reflects data coverage, not actual market share.

Trends are computed from new-construction listings grouped by the period below. Bedroom, bathroom, size, price, and days-on-market fields come directly from the listing record and are reliably populated.
