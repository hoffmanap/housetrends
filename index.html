Market Overview: New Construction vs. Resale

Every sale record is checked against the new-construction listings file by address. A match means that property appeared as a new-construction listing at some point; everything else is treated as resale. Address matching is exact-normalized text, so a small number of true matches may be missed due to formatting differences between sources.

Trends are computed from new-construction listings grouped by the period below. Bedroom, bathroom, size, price, and days-on-market fields come directly from the listing record and are reliably populated. Room count, garage, pool, fireplace, and cooling/heating fields depend on a separate "features" data block that RentCast has not been returning for these listings in practice, so those are omitted here rather than shown mostly blank.
