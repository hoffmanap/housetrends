Market Overview: New Construction vs. Resale

A sale is counted as new construction if it sold the same year it was built or the year after, using the sale record's own yearBuilt field — not by matching against the separate new-construction listings file, which was producing false positives in neighborhoods with no real new construction. One real gap: MLS-backup-sourced sales, the largest slice of this dataset, don't include yearBuilt, so this can only identify new construction where yearBuilt is actually populated (the combined 2019-2025 file, the investor export, and RentCast targeted-pull records). That means new construction is likely undercounted here, not overcounted. When the matched count is small, the actual matched records are listed below the charts so the stat can be checked by eye rather than trusted blind.

Trends are computed from new-construction listings grouped by the period below, using listedDate where available and lastSeenDate as a fallback (RentCast doesn't reliably populate listedDate on older or backfilled listings). Bedroom, bathroom, size, price, and days-on-market fields come directly from the listing record and are reliably populated. Room count, garage, pool, fireplace, and cooling/heating fields depend on a separate "features" data block that RentCast has not been returning for these listings in practice, so those are omitted here rather than shown mostly blank.
